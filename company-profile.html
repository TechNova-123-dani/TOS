Company Profile

Technova ICT Solutions is a forward-thinking technology company based in Narok, Kenya, dedicated to empowering organizations through innovative digital transformation. We specialize in delivering reliable, secure, and scalable ICT solutions that drive efficiency, accelerate growth, and enhance customer engagement. From startups to government institutions, our mission is to bridge the gap between business goals and modern technology.

Our Vision

To become Kenya’s most trusted and impactful ICT partner — transforming businesses through innovative, sustainable, and data-driven solutions that improve lives and inspire progress.

Our Mission

To empower businesses with cutting-edge digital tools, deliver measurable results, and nurture innovation through strategic technology deployment and continuous improvement.

Core Capabilities

  • Web Development: Creating fast, responsive, and modern web solutions that represent brands powerfully.
  • Cybersecurity: Offering penetration testing, system audits, and continuous protection from digital threats.
  • Networking & Cloud: Building secure, scalable network infrastructures and cloud environments.
  • Digital Marketing: Helping brands reach wider audiences with SEO, social campaigns, and analytics.
  • IT Consulting: Providing expert advice for ICT modernization and digital transformation projects.
  • Software Development: Custom-built business software designed to optimize workflows and productivity.

Clients & Partners

Over the years, Technova has partnered with a variety of clients across different sectors — from educational institutions and healthcare providers to financial organizations and NGOs. We pride ourselves in delivering enterprise-grade solutions with a human touch and a deep understanding of local business environments.

Innovation & Research

At the heart of Technova lies Technova ICT Lab — the Home of Innovation. Here, we experiment with emerging technologies like AI, IoT, and data analytics to develop next-generation digital products. This approach ensures our clients benefit from the latest tools that improve efficiency, enhance decision-making, and unlock new business opportunities.

Our Commitment

Every project we undertake is guided by integrity, professionalism, and the pursuit of excellence. We don’t just deliver technology — we deliver trust, growth, and value. Technova remains committed to equipping Africa’s digital future, one solution at a time.

About Technova

Technova is a business technology firm offering web development, cybersecurity, networking and digital marketing services to organizations and SMEs. We build, secure and grow digital products with measurable ROI.
