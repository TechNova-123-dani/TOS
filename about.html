Company Profile

TECHNOVA is a digital innovation company based in Narok, Kenya, offering world-class solutions in web development, cybersecurity, networking, and digital marketing. We specialize in delivering scalable, efficient, and future-ready systems.

  • Founded in 2022 by Daniel Njuguna and Moses Kiprotich.
  • Headquartered in Narok, Kenya with clients across Africa.
  • Focused on youth empowerment through tech education and innovation.
  • Driven by integrity, performance, and community impact.
